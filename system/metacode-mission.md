# MetaCODE Mission

## Purpose

MCA AI.Pack is a system for building, controlling, and evolving AI-driven development workflows.

It is not just a tool.

It is the foundation for MetaCODE Agency products.

---

## Core idea

One system (Main Brain) must:

- create other AI systems
- control their behavior
- enforce architecture and workflow
- prevent degradation and chaos
- scale across multiple projects

---

## Product vision

MetaCODE Agency will deliver:

- AI-powered development systems
- reusable project brains
- structured prompt packs
- controlled frontend/backend pipelines

---

## Main Brain role

AI-brains is:

- the source of truth
- the rule engine
- the architecture guardian
- the system that defines how all other AI agents behave

---

## Long-term goal

Build a system where:

- new projects are created by AI
- development is controlled by rules
- errors are minimized by design
- scaling to multiple teams/projects is safe

---

## Key principle

AI must not be creative by default.

AI must be controlled, structured, and predictable.
